## Interpretation (Claude, for CTO decision; no parameter was changed)
1. **symbolic-0.1 barely discriminates:** σ = 2.9, and 14.5% of time-known pairs land exactly on 50. Its unknown-time ranges are narrow (mean width 0.8–1.8).
2. **symbolic-0.2 has an upward bias on a neutral random corpus:** the mean is 57.5 when time is known. The cause is structural: harmonious polarities (trine +1, sextile +0.6, conjunction +0.5) outweigh tense ones (square −0.7, opposition −0.5). If 50 should mean "no net symbolic tilt", polarities need symmetrising; if not, the UI must say that typical scores sit around 55–60. Decision needed (not tuned here, per CTO C-04).
3. **Missing time lowers 0.2:** both unknown → mean 50.5 and weight coverage 75%. The cause is fewer contacts (no Moon or angles), not "less compatibility". The UI must show coverage next to any score.
4. **Time sensitivity:** ±1 h moves 0.2 by ≥ 5 points in 11.5% of pairs (ASC/MC and overlays), versus 0.3% for 0.1. A wrong birth time matters more in 0.2.
5. **Parameters:**
   - Saturation 1.2–2.0 mainly stretches or compresses the spread (σ 8.5 → 5.9).
   - Neutral hard-conjunction polarity (mixed) shifts the mean by only +0.4.
   - A neutral nodal opposition had no visible effect in this corpus.
6. **Insufficient evidence:** 6.9% of dimensions have no contacts. They are now labelled `insufficient-evidence` instead of being read as neutral 50.
7. **Friendship view:** without the attraction dimension the index moves by up to ±12 (σ 3.9). Friendship should use its own weighting rather than the romantic index.
