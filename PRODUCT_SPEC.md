# Astralis AI — Product specification v0.1

## Promise
Every connection has a story. Discover yours. / Cada conexión tiene una historia. Descubre la tuya.

## MVP
Mobile-first English/Spanish flow: landing → relationship type (partner, crush, friendship) → two birth profiles → free symbolic result → optional share → optional purchase → report. No forced registration before free result. Private by default. Sharing must omit names and natal details by default. Free result includes dimensions, strength, challenge and useful interpretation. Paid premium report adds synastry and discussion prompts. Astrology is symbolic entertainment and reflection, not diagnosis or prediction.

## Catalog (USD, proposed)
Free $0; Identity $4.99; Match Premium $9.99; Couple $14.99; Plus $5.99/month. MVP: Free and Match Premium only. Payments disabled until provider approval and verified sandbox tests.

## Scoring hypotheses
Emotional 25%, communication 20%, attraction 20%, values/stability 20%, growth/challenges 15%. Version rules, normalize, avoid double counting, flag missing data. Scores are symbolic indices, not probabilities. Do not fabricate calculations.

## Goals
1000 users, 10 initial paying customers, $1500 monthly revenue, $1000 operating profit before taxes; $150 validation budget. Goals not guarantees.

## Launch gates
Verified calculation accuracy and license, mobile flow, privacy, secure payments and entitlement, report coherence, tested sharing, funnel and cost measurements.
